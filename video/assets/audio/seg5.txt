One more layer: safety as infrastructure, not as a prompt. Two ADK plugins implement intent-drift detection. The Blue Team logs every tool call into an Agent Bill of Materials and runs a session Trust Score starting at one hundred. Any single node that exceeds ten tool calls, doing far more than its role needs, drops the score by twenty-five. Fall below fifty, and the Green Team quarantines the session. It raises before the next tool call and freezes execution cleanly, preserving full state for forensic review. Prompt instructions can drift; plugin enforcement is deterministic.
