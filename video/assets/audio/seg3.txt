Under the hood, Google ADK orchestrates a workflow graph. The protocol isn't a prompt. It's the topology. Three fan-out and fan-in cycles. The JoinNode barriers make it structurally impossible for any agent to see round two before every round-one response is collected, so the independent evaluation can't be contaminated, even by accident. The graph builds itself at runtime from whichever personas you selected; adding a persona is one registry entry, no wiring. Gemini 2.5 Flash runs the five participants; 2.5 Pro runs the Moderator and Synthesizer, where reasoning quality matters most. Votes come back as type-safe structured output via a Pydantic schema.
